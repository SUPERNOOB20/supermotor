Defined in: <core.h>

In asset is kind of like an image that has already been loaded to RAM! It's a union between SDL_Surface and SDL_Texture.

In the code, it looks like this:

union asset {

      SDL_Surface* surface;

      SDL_Texture* texture;

};

Let's say that you have the following asset:

asset my_asset = ... ;

To render your asset with a CPU:

my_asset.surface

To render your asset with a GPU:

my_asset.texture
